About

Shopify developer focused on fast, accessible stores that convert.

For over six years I’ve been building Shopify stores for brands that want a storefront that looks right and sells well. Most of my work is custom theme development: I take a Figma or PSD design and turn it into a quick, responsive store that matches it down to the pixel.

I’ve delivered projects for international clients and teams in the United States, South Korea and Australia, working remotely across time zones. That experience has made me a clear communicator who keeps projects moving and delivers on schedule.

Beyond themes, I’ve built custom features and add-ons, set up checkout funnels with A/B testing, integrated subscription and other third-party apps, and made storefronts ADA-compliant. I also review code from junior developers, and I care about clean, maintainable work that delivers measurable results.
